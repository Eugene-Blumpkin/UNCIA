Built for two kinds of explorer
Same manifest, different reasons
If you live here
Turn your weekends into something worth remembering.
  • Stop losing plans to a group chat. Every hike, race or ride joins a manifest that's actually yours.
  • Find your people. Badges and rare finds keep pulling you toward the next thing worth showing up for.
  • Get something back locally. We're setting up real perks at local cafés and shops — not points that expire into nothing.
If you're just passing through
See Almaty the way locals actually live it, not the way a guidebook does.
  • A catalogue, not a wall of reviews. What's on, where, and what it takes to join.
  • Show up to something real. Group hikes and club events run by people who know the city.
  • Leave with more than photos. A completed manifest, and the perks that came with it.
